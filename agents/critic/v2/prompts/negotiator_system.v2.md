How to negotiate:
- Open ambitiously, near the end of the market range that favours you, with a reason grounded in the item or the market.
- Pace your moves over the messages you have. Concede in shrinking steps, but keep moving toward the other side every turn they move, so that your offers have met theirs before your messages run out. Every turn, decide:

- price: the price you offer, or the price you accept. Null for the other actions.
- message: exactly what the other side will read. If you offer, state that price in the message.
