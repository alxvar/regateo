# ranged: journal

The record of what was tried on this architecture, version by version: the hypothesis, what changed, the run, and what it showed. Every session that changes this architecture reads it first and adds an entry when it's done. Newest last.

## v1

**2026-10-01. First version of docs/02 O4, with the band from a model, not from code.**

- **Hypothesis:** splitting "where to land" (a strategist that sees the walk-away price) from "what to say" (a negotiator held to a band) bounds the damage the negotiator can do, and the band's width is a single dial for how much we trust it. docs/02 drew the band as computed by code. The user chose to have a strategist call set it, with code only enforcing it (docs/06 §3.3, 2026-10-01).
- **Shape:** per turn, a strategist call (`BandPlan`: read of their latest message, target, best, worst, angle), then `make_band` applies `width` and clamps to the walk-away price, then the negotiator call decides within it. Code vetoes offers outside the band, acceptances of offers worse than `worst`, prices written past the limit and agreement words: one retry, then the offer is clamped into the band with a plain message. A final check never sends a move past the limit.
- **Least privilege:** by default the negotiator never sees the walk-away price, and veto feedback names only the band and the amounts written, never the limit.
- **Configs:** `band` (the strategist's own band), `w0`, `w10`, `w30` (0%, 10%, 30% of the market range around the target), `full` (only the limit binds), `band-sees-limit`. 2 calls per turn.
- **Fixes after the first live matches** (shared code in `lib/common.py`, the same in tools, ranged, strategist and critic):
  - Reading their offer: the amount worst for us in their message picked up competitor quotes ("the other vendor's $72 … meet me at $90" read as $72). Facts now use the last amount of their own in the message. The limit check still reads defensively, except that a message that only restates our price ("Fine, $91") closes at that price. Before the fix, a valid acceptance of $91 was vetoed.
  - A decision that fails its checks twice keeps its own price within the limit, with a plain message ("I can do $150."), instead of restating the opening. The retry now shows the rejected draft.
- **Seen in the smoke matches, not fixed:** in 3 of 4 first matches the agent offered less than the other side had already offered (seller offering $95 against a $130 bid, $108 against $138.78), and once a critic revision held at $125 against $138.78. A veto on offers worse for us than their standing offer would catch it, but that is code deciding when to accept (docs/06 §3.3), so it is left for the user to decide.
- **Run:** dev run `run_01a0f82b4d9d29d2085c` (`engine/configs/gym/archs-v1.yaml`, standard-v2, reference `single_call/v1/baseline`, halving and early stopping). Code uncommitted (recorded as 26bea00a+23ada9b3c971). **Stopped** by us after 539 of 1,632 matches (it would have taken 6–8 more hours, mostly the strategist's thinking); not resumed. Final numbers: `band` −0.042 (p = 0.49, n = 48), cut by halving after 96 pairs. Deals 92% vs 77%, so it closes more often but for less. Gates fail badly: scripted:hardliner −0.434, scripted:liar −0.202. Watch persona:tough −0.122. No deals past the limit.

## v2

**2026-10-01. Round 1 session: concessions tied to their movement (`hold`, `hold-w10`).** New version `v2`, a copy of v1 with the changes below; v1 untouched. Not run live: only `regateo-agent check agents/ranged/v2` (ok) and `pytest agents/ranged` (17 passed) were run (the `.venv/bin` shims point at a missing interpreter here, so both were run through `python`).

- **Failure targeted:** in the 96 logged matches v1 gives away its range in big steps whatever the other side does. Against `scripted:hardliner` (share 0.077 vs the reference's 0.511, the worst gate) the opponent's offers barely move for four or five rounds (e.g. seller, our limit $91.9: they say $64, $64, $64, $68, $84; we go $145, $130, $120, $98, $95 and close at $95, share 0.085). Our offers fall by $15–25 per turn while theirs sit still, and 15 of 16 hardliner matches closed (a buyer at $128.2 on a $128.2 limit, share 0). The same shape shows against `scripted:liar` (0.144) and `persona:tough` (0.162: our offers walk toward our limit and we close within a few dollars of it in most deals). The strategist prompt asked to "concede slowly… most of the moving near the end", but the strategist was given no count of who had moved how far. The logs do not hold the strategist's plans, so how wide its bands were is not known. A second, smaller failure: the mentions veto fires on nearly every turn against low-ball offers (their $64 is past a seller's limit), and after two vetoes the repair sends a bare "I can do $120." with no argument.
- **Changes (both variants):** (1) strategist prompt `strategist_system.v2`: concede only when they moved since our last offer and by less than they did; hold otherwise; no big move while they have barely moved; their walk-away price is probably well beyond their offers; time matters only in the last two messages; keep `worst` off the limit until the last message. (2) Code adds a ledger of facts to the strategist's input (our offers, their offers, how far each has moved, messages left); code states facts only and sets no price. (3) The negotiator prompts say to refer to "your offer" rather than repeat their price, to cut vetoes. Param `hold` (default true; `hold: false` gives v1's strategist, though the negotiator prompt change stays).
- **Variant `ranged/v2/hold`** (`extends: ranged/v1/band`, `hold: true`). *Hypothesis:* the loss is unreciprocated concession by the strategist, and a ledger plus a reciprocity rule fixes it without code picking prices. *Expected:* hardliner and liar gates recover to within 0.10 of the reference, tough improves, deal rate falls somewhat from 92%. *Wrong if:* the hardliner share stays below about 0.3 (the strategist ignores the ledger), or the share rises only with deals dropping enough to lose overall (no-deal is 0), or the mean share against the three LLM personas is no better than v1's 0.386.
- **Variant `ranged/v2/hold-w10`** (`extends: ranged/v2/hold`, `width: 0.1`). *Hypothesis:* the negotiator may also concede more than the strategist intends inside a wide band (untested: the plans are not in the logs), so narrowing to 10% of the market range around the strategist's target adds to `hold`. *Wrong if:* it is no better than `hold` (then the strategist alone is the dial), or worse on the personas because a narrow band blocks the negotiator from accepting or from following a real deadline.
- **Not done:** an offer worse than their standing offer (v1 smoke matches) is still not vetoed, as that is code choosing when to accept. A seller's worst case remains a late acceptance at the limit.

**Fixed by hand after collect, before the round's run:** v2's facts read our own offers with `lib.common.our_offers`, which looks only at the move's action and price. On free text the platform drops both from our own messages (what we meant is in `meta["intent"]`), so the facts said "your offers so far: none" every turn. v2 now reads them with its own `offers.py` (intent first, then the logged decision), tested in `tests/test_offers.py`. The same bug is in v1 (and in every version's `safe_move`, which therefore restates the opening price instead of our last offer: the "My offer stands at $68" seen in the data); v1 and `lib/` stay as they are.

## v3

**2026-10-02. The veto on offers worse than their standing offer, behind a switch (`hold-standing`).** New version `v3`, a copy of v2 with the change below; v2 and `lib/` untouched. Not run live: `regateo-agent check agents/ranged/v3` (ok) and `pytest agents/ranged` (31 passed).

- **Failure targeted:** offers worse for us than the other side's standing offer, left open in v1 and v2 for the user to decide. In round-01's dev run (`run_01a0f9180ca60d701354`), `ranged/v2/hold` made one in 15% of its matches (e.g. buyer 8-1-b: they asked $91, the strategist's band was $132–140 and we bid $135). The user approved the veto on 2026-10-02, behind a switch.
- **Change:** param `standing` (default false, so v3 without it behaves as v2). When on, the negotiator's check vetoes an offer worse for us than their standing offer, with the SDK's `agent_sdk.guards.standing_problems`: "their latest offer, $X, is already better for you than your offer of $Y. Accept their offer, or offer a price better for you than $X." Which of the two stays with the model. Their offer is read defensively (`offers.their_floor`): of the amounts of their own in their latest message that names one, the worst for us, so a quoted or planted amount can't set it off. The strategist's band is unchanged: when it leaves no room for a better counter, accepting is the only move that passes.
- **Variant `ranged/v3/hold-standing`** (`extends: ranged/v2/hold`, `standing: true`). *Hypothesis:* the offers worse than theirs are slips of reading, not strategy, so the veto turns them into acceptances or better counters and gains share with no cost elsewhere. *Expected:* such offers drop to near 0% (`regateo signals`), more deals within the limit, share up against the LLM personas. *Wrong if:* the deal rate or share falls (the veto pushes it into accepting worse deals it would have improved on), or the repair still sends the dominated price (the model ignores the feedback twice).
- **Run:** none yet. It is a challenger in `engine/configs/gym/redteam-01.yaml`; on dev, compare it with `ranged/v2/hold`, its same agent with the switch off.
